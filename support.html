How do I add an expense?

Open the Expenses tab and tap the + button. Enter a name, amount, category, and frequency, then save. Add only the expenses you want to track.

How do savings suggestions work?

Finiteck analyses the expenses you add and suggests practical changes. Optional cloud AI and web search can help explore alternatives, with your consent. Suggestions are not guaranteed savings; always check the provider’s current terms.

Is my data kept on my device?

Your financial records are stored locally. Optional cloud AI can send selected expense information to external providers, with your consent. On-device analysis works offline. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to Profile > Subscription and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple ID associated with your original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Finiteck to manage or cancel your plan. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I export or delete my data?

In Finiteck, go to Profile > Data. Use Export Data to save a copy. Delete All Data permanently removes your local records, so export anything you want to keep first.

How do I change the language?

Go to Profile > Settings and choose a language. Finiteck supports English, French, Romanian, and German.

How do I report a problem?

Email us with a description, steps to reproduce it, and your device model and iOS version. If you include a screenshot, hide any financial or personal information you do not want to share.
