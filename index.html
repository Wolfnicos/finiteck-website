Before you get started

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Something else on your mind?

We’re here to help
Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Add the bills and subscriptions you want to track yourself. Finiteck never asks for your bank login details.

What stays on my device?

Your expenses are stored locally. On-device analysis works offline. Optional cloud AI and web search can send selected expense details to AI providers, only with your consent. You can review the details in our privacy policy. Read our privacy policy.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Continue with a monthly subscription at €3.99 or an annual subscription at €39.99. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Apple shows the applicable price and terms before purchase.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Finiteck works on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app supports English, French, Romanian, and German.

Are the savings guaranteed?

No. Suggestions help you explore alternatives; actual savings depend on your expenses and the offers available to you. Verify current prices and conditions with the provider before making a change.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select Finiteck. You manage and cancel your subscription directly through Apple.
