# Search and media audit — 2026-10-09

## Implemented

- Checked all 76 previously external article images: all downloaded and decoded successfully.
- Preserved those illustrations, hosted them on finiteck.com, resized large originals to at most 1600 px, and encoded WebP. Total bytes changed from 27,030,932 to 8,458,728 (68.7% smaller).
- Encoded the three real app screenshots as WebP: 185,776 bytes combined instead of 485,125 bytes. Original JPEGs remain available. Corrected their intrinsic dimensions to 777 × 1688.
