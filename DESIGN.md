# Finiteck visual system

## Direction

A quiet, precise companion for someone reviewing monthly subscriptions on their phone at the end of the day. Continue the app's dark charcoal surface and warm gold identity. Use Manrope, chosen for its open numerals and compact, geometric letterforms, with pronounced display/body scale contrast.

## Palette

- Background: #111310, warm charcoal.
- Raised surface: #191c17; borders: #34392f.
- Primary text: #f4f2e9; secondary: #b0b5a7.
- Brand accent: #dfc17a, muted warm gold.
- Accent surface: #e9d69e, used for the interactive calculator.
- Positive data: #b8d7a0; never the only signal of meaning.

## Typography and layout

Self-hosted Manrope variable, 400–800, with system fallback. Display type uses fluid sizes, tight tracking, and balanced wrapping. Reading content is limited to approximately 70 characters. Main container: 1200px. Main section spacing: fluid 72–128px. Use lines and whitespace to organize content; cards are reserved for distinct interactions or linked resources.

## Components

Shared static header/footer, native details mobile menu, gold download CTA, real screenshots in a simple device frame, step selector with visible content fallback, browser-local recurring-cost calculator, two pricing choices, search/filter journal, article contents navigation, and a branded 404.

## Motion and accessibility

Short opacity/transform entrance and hover transitions. Never hide content pending JavaScript. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No custom cursor, scroll hijacking, autoplay carousel, or background video. Controls have visible focus and a 44px target where practical.

## Assets

Existing app logo retained. Product screenshots downloaded from Finiteck's public App Store listing; label sample data. Font served locally with SIL Open Font License. No new third-party runtime dependencies.
